## 1. Objective

Establish a reproducible, quantitative baseline for normal syslog events collected from EDGE-R1 and SERVER-1 by the centralized TELEMETRY-1 collector.

The experiment addresses WP01 finding F03 by replacing an undocumented event-rate assumption with a measured observation supported by preserved research evidence.

## 2. Experimental Environment

| Component | Role | IP Address |
|---|---|---|
| EDGE-R1 | Network router and syslog source | 192.168.30.1 |
| SERVER-1 | Server and syslog source | 192.168.20.10 |
| TELEMETRY-1 | Centralized syslog collector | 192.168.30.10 |

The experiment was conducted within the RC-002 GNS3 laboratory.

## 3. Methodology

A controlled 60-minute observation window was selected to measure routine syslog events received from the two monitored devices.

**Observation window:**

- Recorded start: `2026-10-19T13:38:50Z`
- Calculated end: `2026-10-19T14:38:50Z`
- Duration: 60 minutes
- Interval convention: Start inclusive, end exclusive

The start timestamp was recorded during the experiment. The end timestamp was calculated from the start time and specified duration rather than captured independently at the end boundary.

Syslog records were extracted from the collector's remote log files using timestamp-based filtering.

**Collection sources:**

- EDGE-R1: `/var/log/remote/192.168.30.1/syslog.log`
- SERVER-1: `/var/log/remote/192.168.20.10/syslog.log`

The analysis counted matching records within the selected observation window. It did not attempt to infer unobserved events.

| EDGE-R1 | 5 | 60 minutes | 5 events/hour |
| SERVER-1 | 5 | 60 minutes | 5 events/hour |
| **Combined** | **10** | **60 minutes** | **10 events/hour** |

### 4.2 Rate Calculation

Observed event rate = Number of collected events / Observation duration in hours.

- EDGE-R1: 5 / 1 = **5 events/hour**
- SERVER-1: 5 / 1 = **5 events/hour**
- Combined: 10 / 1 = **10 events/hour**

These values describe the events present in the collector's retained logs during this specific observation window.

## 6. Limitations

1. **Single observation window:** The measurement represents one 60-minute trial and does not establish a statistically representative long-term baseline.

2. **Limited event diversity:** All ten collected records were routine cron-related events. Other operational event types may occur at different rates.

3. **Collection versus generation:** The measured rates reflect events present in the centralized collector, not necessarily every event generated by the source devices.

4. **Unverified delivery completeness:** No source-side event-generation counter or independent packet-level delivery measurement was used to calculate collection completeness.

5. **Timestamp interpretation:** The analysis used timestamps recorded in the collected syslog entries. These should not be interpreted as independently verified collector-arrival timestamps.

6. **Earlier interrupted trial:** A previous observation attempt was interrupted by a GNS3 shutdown and was excluded from the quantitative results.

## 7. Research Interpretation

The experiment establishes an evidence-backed reference measurement for low-activity, routine syslog collection in the RC-002 laboratory.

Within the observed 60-minute interval:

- EDGE-R1 contributed five collected events.
- SERVER-1 contributed five collected events.
- The centralized collector retained ten matching events in total.

This measurement can support subsequent experiments comparing event volumes under controlled changes.

However, a single trial is insufficient to define a stable long-term distribution, determine variability, or establish reliable anomaly-detection thresholds.
